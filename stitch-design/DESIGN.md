## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of computational engineering and deliberate, tactile art direction. Created for Muhyideen Abdulbasit, the aesthetic rejects the sterilized, clinical tropes of typical developer portfolios in favor of an organic-architectural editorial presence. 

The visual style blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Brutalism**. High typographic contrast pairs sculpted display typography with precise monospace metrics. The palette evokes physical materials: sun-baked parchment, soot-rubbed volcanic ash, raw iron, and terracotta pigment. Surfaces feel crafted rather than synthesized. 

The interface communicates intellect, meticulous craftsmanship, deliberate curiosity, and unwavering execution. It invites peers, collaborators, and art directors to read portfolio artifacts as published essays rather than disposable product showcases.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from classical print media, scorched clay, and dark volcanic rock. It operates predominantly in light mode with deep, rich charcoal anchoring its structure.

- **Primary Accent (`#D95D39`, `terracotta-accent`):** An assertive, earth-fired burnt orange used sparingly for active states, semantic cues, focus rings, and high-impact editorial callouts.
- **Secondary Base (`#1F1E1D`, `ash-charcoal`):** A warm soot black used for primary headlines, prominent borders, high-contrast badges, and deep surface blocks.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#C8502C`, `terracotta-deep`):** A darker, oxidized rust used for interactive hover states, active links, and deep contrast accents against light parchment.
- **Canvas Neutrals (`#FBF9F5`, `#F5F1EA`, `#EAE5DC`):** Organic, paper-grain surfaces. Content lives across layered paper tones to create rhythm without artificial drop shadows.
- **Ash Neutral (`#171615`):** The absolute grounding value for high-density body typography and structural divider lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates tension between artistic human expression and mathematical precision:

1. **Display & Headings (Syne):** Syne commands large scale with its sculptural geometric quirks and editorial boldness. Used for project names, essay titles, and hero statements.
2. **Body Prose (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A friendly yet crisp geometric grotesque that provides effortless reading clarity across case studies and architecture notes.
3. **Labels & Metadata (Space Mono):** Monospaced technical indicators anchor the engineering pedigree. Used strictly for dates, commit hashes, technical stacks, architecture diagrams, and sub-headers. Always set with positive letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) and uppercase styling where appropriate.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model pairs classical architectural proportions with modern fluid modularity:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column asymmetric fluid grid with `3.5rem` outer canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Tablet adjusts to 8 columns with `2rem` margins. Mobile falls back to a 4-column flow with `1.25rem` margins.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous macro-spacing separates project entries, giving each system artifact room to breathe. Component padding strictly follows the mathematical scale (`space-xs` through `space-xl`).
- **Asymmetrical Compositions:** Hero zones and case studies avoid static centered layouts, using intentional offsets (e.g., column spans 1–7 for narrative and 8–12 for code/technical specifications).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurry digital drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Tiering and Low-Contrast Tactile Outlines**:

- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation is communicated via background shifts from `#FBF9F5` (base canvas) to `#F5F1EA` (tier 1 cards/modules) to `#EAE5DC` (tier 2 inset blocks).
- **Physical Inset Borders:** Structural divisions utilize delicate, ink-like borders (`1px solid rgba(23, 22, 21, 0.12)`). Active elements switch to `#D95D39`.
- **Hard Surface Offset (Deliberate Tactility):** When physical separation is required (e.g., featured project highlights), use a hard, unblurred shadow offset: `4px 4px 0px #1F1E1D` instead of a diffused blur. This creates an authentic printmaking aesthetic.

## Shapes

The geometric personality is sharp and disciplined, reflecting architectural precision:

- A roundedness factor of `1` (Soft) produces crisp `0.25rem` corners for cards, input fields, and standard container boxes.
- Interactive tags, pills, and status chips retain `0.25rem` corners or strict right angles (`0px`) for a technical blueprint feel.
- Pure circular borders are reserved strictly for avatar rings, status dots, and icon boundary capsules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#1F1E1D` with `#FBF9F5` text. On hover, background transitions to `#D95D39` with an instantaneous `150ms` ease. Includes subtle `0.25rem` radius and `label-code` typography.
- **Outline / Editorial:** Transparent background with `1px solid rgba(23, 22, 21, 0.2)` border. On hover, shifts to `1px solid #1F1E1D` accompanied by a `4px 4px 0px #1F1E1D` hard offset shadow.
- **Ghost:** Text-only in `#171615` with an underline positioned `4px` beneath text, transitioning to `#D95D39` upon interaction.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Built with `Space Mono` typography (`label-meta`).
- Background `#F5F1EA`, border `1px solid rgba(23, 22, 21, 0.12)`, padding `0.25rem 0.5rem`.
- Active or highlighted tags use `#D95D39` text with a `rgba(217, 93, 57, 0.08)` fill.

### Cards (Case Studies & Systems)
- Grounded on `#F5F1EA` surfaces with a 1px border (`rgba(23, 22, 21, 0.1)`).
- Interior padding `1.75rem` (`space-lg`).
- On hover, the container shifts subtly up by `2px` with a crisp `4px 4px 0px #1F1E1D` hard edge shadow and a `#D95D39` border highlight.

### Input Fields
- Understated architectural inputs: Background `#FBF9F5`, bottom-only or thin all-around border (`1px solid rgba(23, 22, 21, 0.2)`).
- Focus states invoke a `1px solid #D95D39` outline with no glowing blurred halo. Placeholder text set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` muted ash tone (`rgba(23, 22, 21, 0.45)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square elements with `0.125rem` radius or clean circles.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid rgba(23, 22, 21, 0.35)`.
- Checked: `#D95D39` background with an off-white inner indicator.

### Monospace Spec Blocks (Custom Product Component)
- Distinctive metadata blocks for software projects displaying metrics like `STACK`, `ROLE`, `YEAR`, and `GITHUB COMMIT`.
- Features alternating shaded rows (`#FBF9F5` and `#F5F1EA`) wrapped in a single `1px solid rgba(23, 22, 21, 0.12)` boundary, set exclusively in `Space Mono`.
